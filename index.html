What Is GoG ePay Services?

GoG ePay Services is an electronic payslip service associated with the Government of Ghana payroll system. http://gogepaysslip.com/ The Controller and Accountant-General’s Department (CAGD) oversees government payroll administration and has introduced an upgraded electronic payslip system for eligible public sector employees.

The service provides a digital way to access salary information using an internet-connected phone or computer. Depending on the account and features available, employees may be able to review salary statements, earnings, deductions, and other payroll details.

The terms GoG payslip, GoG ePayslip, and GoG ePay Services are commonly used when discussing this electronic salary information service.

How to Access the GoG Payslip Login Portal

Start by opening the current GoG ePay Services portal. Confirm that you are on the correct website before entering personal or employment information.

  1. Open a trusted web browser on your phone or computer.
  2. Visit the current GoG ePay Services login portal.
  3. Enter the registered employee and account information requested.
  4. Complete any security or verification steps shown on the page.
  5. Open your dashboard after your details have been accepted.
  6. Navigate to the payslip section and choose the relevant pay period.
Security tip: Do not enter your login details on unfamiliar websites or share your password or one-time password with anyone claiming to provide account support.

How to Register for GoG ePay Services

Employees who have not registered may need to create an account before accessing their electronic payslips. Requirements depend on the current system and the employee’s payroll record. The upgraded process includes employee information and Ghana Card or NIA verification.

1. Open the current portal

Visit the current GoG ePay Services portal and find the registration option. Read the on-screen instructions before starting.

2. Enter your employee details

Provide your employee number and the other requested employment information. Enter the details accurately so the system can locate the correct payroll record.

3. Complete identity and phone verification

Follow the Ghana Card verification instructions and confirm your phone number when required. A one-time password (OTP) may be sent to verify the number. Never share this code with another person.

4. Create your account credentials

Create a secure password and set up the security question or other recovery details requested by the portal. Review your information before submitting the registration form.

How to Check and Download Your GoG ePayslip

After signing in, open the payslip section on your dashboard. The available records and download options may vary depending on your account and payroll period.

Basic salary and allowances

Review your main salary amount and any applicable additional payments.

Deductions

Check the deductions and payroll adjustments listed for the selected period.

Net salary

Review the amount remaining after applicable deductions are applied.

Download or print

If the portal offers the option, save a copy for your personal records.

Select the correct month and year, open the relevant statement, and check the information carefully. Store downloaded payslips securely because they contain confidential employment and financial details.

If an expected payslip is missing, confirm the selected period and contact your payroll office if the record remains unavailable.

Understanding Salary Information on Your Payslip

Reviewing a payslip regularly helps you understand how your monthly salary is calculated.

  • Basic salary: The main salary amount associated with your position and applicable pay structure.
  • Allowances: Additional payments that may apply according to employment terms and eligibility.
  • Deductions: Applicable taxes, pension contributions, and other authorized deductions.
  • Net salary: The amount remaining after relevant deductions have been applied to earnings.

Compare new figures with previous statements. If you notice an unexpected deduction or incorrect employment information, contact your HR or payroll department for clarification.

How to Recover Your GoG Payslip Login

If you forget your password, start by opening the login page and selecting the available password recovery option. Follow the instructions and provide the employee or verification details requested by the portal.

Depending on the current recovery process, you may need to confirm your phone number or answer a security question set during registration.

What if you do not receive the OTP?

Check your mobile network and confirm that the registered phone number is correct. Wait briefly before requesting another code. If recovery still fails, contact the appropriate payroll office or official CAGD support channel.

Common GoG Payslip Login Problems

Login issues may result from incorrect details, incomplete registration, identity verification failures, browser problems, or temporary portal interruptions.

  • Check that your employee details and password are correct.
  • Confirm that you have completed the required registration steps.
  • Make sure your internet connection is stable.
  • Refresh the page or try an updated browser.
  • Clear the browser cache if the page continues to behave unexpectedly.
  • Use the official recovery process if you cannot remember your password.

If the issue continues, contact your payroll department or the official support channel. Avoid services that promise to unlock accounts in exchange for passwords or verification codes.

Can You Access GoG ePayslip on a Mobile Phone?

Eligible employees can generally use a compatible mobile browser to access web-based payslip services, subject to the current portal’s requirements.

Open your browser, visit the correct portal, and sign in with your registered credentials. If you download a payslip, check your Downloads folder or the location where your browser saves files. You can keep the document for personal reference or print it when needed.

When using a shared device, do not save your password and remember to sign out after viewing your account.

GoG ePayslip and Salary-Backed Loans

Some payroll portals may provide features related to salary-backed loan verification, such as generating a mandate number. Availability depends on the current system and your account. If you need this feature, check the options displayed in the official portal and confirm the requirements with your bank or relevant payroll office.

Never share account credentials or OTP codes with a third party as part of a loan application.
